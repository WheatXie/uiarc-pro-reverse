# Playful

## `orbit-logos` — Orbit logos (Pro, special)

An integrations showcase with brand logos orbiting your product mark on tilted rings. Hover or focus one to rest the orbit, draw a connection, and see what it does.

When to use:
- An integrations or ecosystem hero on a marketing site.
- An integrations settings screen where people browse, read about, and connect tools.

When not to use:
- Use a logo marquee for a long list of customer logos.
- Use a searchable grid or table for catalogs larger than about twenty integrations.

Keywords: special, motion, integrations, logos, new, orbit logos, integration hero, logo orbit, orbiting logos, integrations page, connect apps, ecosystem, partners, tech stack

Files: demo.tsx, orbit-logos.module.css, orbit-logos.tsx

## `page-curl` — Page curl (Pro, special)

A small magazine you read by dragging page corners, with a real fold, lit curl, and flick to turn.

When to use:
- Editorial and brand showcases where paper is part of the story.
- Menus, catalogs, and yearbooks with a handful of spreads.

When not to use:
- Use carousel or a scrolling page for long content or content people search.
- Use a document viewer for dense text that needs zoom and selection.

Keywords: special, gesture, book, new, page curl, page flip, flipbook, turn page animation, book component, magazine viewer, react page flip, page turn

Files: demo.tsx, page-curl.module.css, page-curl.tsx

## `prize-wheel` — Prize wheel (Pro, special)

A referral or promo prize wheel you flick to spin, with real angular momentum, a flexible pointer that ticks against each peg, weighted segments set by the host, a slow suspenseful settle, and the won segment lifting into a reward card with a copyable code.

When to use:
- A referral or launch page that rewards a signup with a discount code.
- An in-product moment that grants one of several perks, such as a milestone reward.

When not to use:
- Use a plain coupon card or copy button when everyone gets the same reward.
- Avoid it in serious flows such as billing or checkout, where chance feels out of place.

Keywords: special, gesture, physics, new, prize wheel, spin the wheel, wheel of fortune, referral, promo code, coupon, reward, giveaway, gamification, spinner, momentum

Files: demo.tsx, prize-wheel.module.css, prize-wheel.tsx

## `sticker-peel` — Sticker peel (Pro, special)

Peel stickers off a surface with a real curl, move them around, and stick them back down.

When to use:
- A personal or team page where people arrange badges, achievements, or brand stickers.
- A launch or marketing moment that invites people to play with the product's marks.

When not to use:
- Use reorderable-list or a sortable grid when the order of items carries meaning.
- Use note-stack for content people need to read and rearrange.

Keywords: special, motion, new, sticker peel, peel effect, sticker drag, page curl, laptop stickers, draggable stickers, react sticker, peel off animation

Files: demo.tsx, sticker-peel.module.css, sticker-peel.tsx
