# Cursor

## `dot-grid` — Dot grid (Pro, special)

A dot field that bulges around the cursor like a lens and ripples from every click.

When to use:
- A landing page hero that should feel alive without video.
- A background for an empty state, a sign in panel, or a launch countdown.

When not to use:
- Behind dense text or data; the moving dots compete with reading.
- Use a static CSS dot pattern when no interaction is wanted.

Keywords: special, motion, new, dot grid, interactive dot background, dot field, magnetic dots, ripple background, hero background, canvas dots, lens effect

Files: demo.tsx, dot-grid.module.css, dot-grid.tsx

## `gravity-well` — Gravity well (Pro, special)

A grid of tiles that bends toward the cursor like spacetime, with a click that sends a shockwave through it.

When to use:
- A team, customer, or moodboard wall where playfulness is the point.
- Landing page sections that show breadth: many logos, avatars, or photos at once.

When not to use:
- Grids people must scan or click precisely, like file pickers; use a plain grid.
- Large collections; use infinite-moodboard or photo-grid.

Keywords: special, grid, physics, new, gravity, spacetime, magnetic, lens, shockwave, ripple, tiles, springs

Files: demo.module.css, demo.tsx, gravity-well.module.css, gravity-well.tsx

## `image-trail` — Image trail (Pro, special)

Moving the pointer leaves a curated trail of photos that pop in, lean, and fade away.

When to use:
- A portfolio or agency hero that should feel alive on first move.
- Travel, food, or product stories told through a sequence of photos.

When not to use:
- Use photo-grid or lightbox-gallery when people need to look at each photo.
- Use hover-image-list to preview one image per row in a list.

Keywords: special, media, cursor, new, image trail, cursor image trail, mouse trail photos, hover photo trail, portfolio hero effect, image cursor follow, react image trail

Files: demo.module.css, demo.tsx, image-trail.module.css, image-trail.tsx

## `ink-fluid` — Ink fluid (Pro, special)

A real time fluid simulation behind your hero: the cursor stirs soft ink that swirls, settles and fades; calm when left alone.

When to use:
- A landing hero or sign in screen that should feel alive without a video.
- Empty states and waiting screens where a gentle, interactive background rewards curiosity.

When not to use:
- Dense product surfaces such as tables and forms. Movement behind data distracts.
- More than one per page; each instance runs its own simulation.

Keywords: special, background, webgl, new, fluid simulation, ink, smoke, hero background, stable fluids, interactive background, cursor effect, navier stokes

Files: demo.tsx, ink-fluid.module.css, ink-fluid.tsx
